# Fruity Net! Happy Harvest — Lasso & Recipe Edition

Draw a closed loop with your finger or mouse to catch fruits inside it.
Return near the start to close the net. Tapping alone does not capture fruit.
Large loops are limited, so choose a small cluster carefully.

Fill the fruit recipe card at the top of the screen. Only requested fruit
counts toward deliveries. Wrong fruits cost time; rotten fruit costs a heart.
Golden fruits act as a wildcard for an outstanding order item.

Complete every order to clear the level and unlock the next. There are
30 levels, five pastel worlds, increasingly complex fruit movements,
stars, combos, Rainbow Fever, music, and local save progress.

On smartphones, draw with one finger. On desktop, hold the left mouse
button and draw a loop; release near where you began.
Press P or Esc to pause; Enter or Space starts or advances a level.

Original illustrated 17 fruit characters, background worlds, and music.
The browser build is HTML5 Canvas; the Godot source remains in the
authoring project. No ads, accounts, or third-party trackers.
